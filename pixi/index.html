01 — The pace

It reads
before it likes.

5
likes a day, by default

Hinge runs in the Android emulator on your Windows PC. For each profile the tool scrolls back to the top, then down through it with a person's unevenness: random distance, random speed, an occasional scroll back, a pause to read.

In ask mode it stops and waits for y, n or q. In auto mode it pauses four to twelve seconds as if reading, then likes. Both stop at the daily cap, and the cap holds across runs: it is counted from your own log.

02 — The words

Your words,
its eyes,
or silence.

Every like can carry one line. In ask mode you see it first and keep it, rewrite it, or send the like on its own.

The vision model sees the phone screen with the chosen photo's like button circled, and writes about that photo only. Its reply is reduced to printable ASCII before a single key is typed.

03 — The hand

Touch,
not injection.

  • Input deviceemulator touchscreen
  • Pressure150 – 500 of 1024
  • Contact size1200 – 2200, oval
  • Samples per tap3 – 7
  • Offset from targetup to 12 px
  • Thumb restsx 90–260 · x 820–1000
  • Typinglog-normal rhythm
Press and hold · the longer, the firmer

adb shell input tap arrives from a virtual device, with pressure 1.0 and no contact size. hinge.py sends raw multi-touch events through the emulator console instead, so each one lands on the same touchscreen a mouse click on the window would, with a finger's roll, weight and shape.

Pixels are read from the emulator window on your desktop. Nothing runs inside the phone.

04 — What it will not do

Restraint,
by design.

Every step is checked against a fresh screenshot. On a screen it does not expect, it stops before the next tap rather than guessing.

  • Never taps a Rose, a Boost, HingeX, or anything that costs money.
  • Never sends a message in a chat.
  • Never runs in the background or on a schedule.
  • Never changes your profile, settings or account.
  • Never passes the daily cap, and Stop always wins.
  • Keeps what it saves about other people on your machine, out of git.

Automating Hinge is against its Terms of Service and can get your account banned. Use it on your own account, at your own risk. This project is not affiliated with Hinge or Match Group.
